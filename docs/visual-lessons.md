# Visual lessons — version 1.7.0

All **269 curriculum teaching/review sessions** across 7A, 7B and Grade 8 now open with a visual challenge. There are 576 illustrated teaching frames, using 24 reusable diagram and activity types. Both shared starter lessons keep their existing visuals.

## In class

- Use the opening prompt to predict, notice, compare or explain.
- **Play / Stop** starts and stops purposeful motion on process illustrations.
- **Next focus** steps through four teaching prompts. It never advances the lesson.
- **Enlarge** opens a larger board view. Close or Escape returns to the same visual.
- **Reset** resets the visual activity only.
- In runoff activities, change soil/paving and add rain to compare the qualitative model.
- In budget activities, choose options and compare their total with the source budget. Discuss benefits and trade-offs, not just cost.

Water-cycle, moving-ice, river-flow, route and connection illustrations use motion. Source, perspective and chart activities use focus steps instead of unnecessary continuous motion. All motion starts stopped. Attention, Pause and other teacher panels stop it; restarting motion requires Play. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation while retaining focus steps and interactions.

Visual focus, selected options and model toggles are temporary presentation tools. They reset when the teaching frame is rerendered or reopened; they are not student results. Existing stage/frame progress, discussion tracking and teacher-controlled answer reveals keep their original IDs and storage format.

## Content and scope

Original vectors are rendered locally as SVG. Source passages stay available alongside illustrations; longer passages keep their full reading width. Named diagrams and curated charts use lesson quantities, with denominators and data limitations shown where relevant. Place and process illustrations are schematic, not photographs or surveyed maps. The runoff activity is qualitative and is explicitly not a flood forecast.

GP includes perspective conversations, source investigations, linked systems, a clothing supply chain, real lesson-data charts and budget decisions. Geography includes UK location, settlement patterns, weather, grids, coordinates, distance, profiles, glaciers, rivers and the water cycle.

Exams and quiz-answer frames do not receive these interactive visuals. Printable student papers and keys retain their existing format. No new runtime packages, external image downloads, network services, audio or student accounts are introduced.

## Verification

npm run test:visuals checks all illustrated frames with a visible timer at 1280 × 720, controls and unchanged lesson position, enlarged-view recovery, Attention, runoff comparisons, budgets, reduced motion and responsive layouts. Screenshots of diagrams, perspective scenes, lesson-data charts and budget choices are visually reviewed. Existing classroom and lesson-selection/printing workflows provide regression coverage.
