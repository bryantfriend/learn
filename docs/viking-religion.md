# Viking Religion: Gods, Stories and Evidence

40-minute substitute lesson for Grades 7–8. Open the main page’s **Viking religion · 40 min** link, select the class, then Start lesson. Also available under Religion · Substitute lessons. Uses the platform’s existing Attention, Timer, reveal, teacher notes and saved-progress controls.

No preparation, worksheets, student devices or external videos required. Students can respond orally or in their existing notebooks. Teach about religion respectfully; no student needs to disclose personal beliefs or take part in worship.

Timing: starter 4 min; goals and vocabulary 3; gods 7; stories 5; daily practices 6; evidence 6; religious change 5; exit 4. Teacher notes and answer explanations are included in the player. If time is short, use one partner response per section. If time remains, challenge “all Vikings believed the same thing.”

Exit success: identify a god and association; explain a practice and possible purpose; identify a source limitation; describe changing beliefs.

## Historical sources

- [National Museum of Denmark: The old religion](https://en.natmus.dk/historical-knowledge/denmark/prehistoric-period-until-1050-ad/the-viking-age/religion-magic-death-and-rituals/the-old-religion/) — Norse beliefs and the limits of surviving evidence.
- [National Museum of Denmark: Religion, magic, death and rituals](https://en.natmus.dk/historical-knowledge/denmark/prehistoric-period-until-1050-ad/the-viking-age/religion-magic-death-and-rituals/) — religious practices and Christianity during the Viking Age.
- [British Museum: Viking visit guide](https://www.britishmuseum.org/sites/default/files/2019-09/Visit_Vikings_KS2_FINAL.pdf) — gods and encounters with Christianity.

The lesson summarises these sources in original classroom language. All six generated images are modern illustrations, not archaeological photographs or evidence of a particular event. The gods and world tree depict mythology. The community scenes are plausible reconstructions; beliefs and practices varied.
