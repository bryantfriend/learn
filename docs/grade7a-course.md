## Printing assessments

Open an assessment and choose **Print student paper**, or use the [assessment index](../exams/). Each 7A paper contains 12 questions, four options each, one mark per answer. Student pages contain no answer explanations. Teacher tools opens a separate explained key.

Print / Save as PDF uses four A4 pages per paper or key. These are teacher-authored classroom assessments, not official external examination papers. MCQs check knowledge and reasoning; observation and students' work are still needed for collaboration, research and speaking skills.

No student scores or personal information are stored by the app. Lesson progress remains separate for every class, subject and lesson in the current browser.
