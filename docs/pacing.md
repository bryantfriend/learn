# Lesson pacing

Curriculum teaching lessons have a 40-minute core and two optional five-minute reserve tasks in **More practice +**. Attendance and announcements are not included. The 25 assessment sessions and two classroom-routine lessons retain their own structure.

The 253 standard teaching lessons now use short two-minute openings, separate evidence-recording and explanation tasks, independent responses, peer review and comparison. Each step has a timer estimate whose total matches its stage. The 7A GP Lesson 2 custom sequence also has revised timings, a new canteen case, a comparison of two plans, and two authored reserve challenges. The other 15 custom visual lessons retain their authored core sequences and receive reserve access. Estimates are planning aids, not measured classroom durations or an instruction to stretch teacher talk.

Reserve tasks reuse each lesson's actual source. Students produce an improved explanation or design and solve a source-based challenge. Grade 8 GP Lesson 2 has two additional authored supply-chain cases (fabric stocks and conflicting needs). These do not change the core checkpoint or mark a discussion complete. Opening reserve work pauses the core timer; closing it returns to the same frame, paused.

If a class finishes the core early, use one or both reserve tasks before the final reflection. Collect a visible response from everyone before moving on, but do not make pupils wait for a timer if their work already demonstrates understanding. Observe actual timings and adjust subsequent teaching; no fixed plan can guarantee identical duration in every class.

Standard lesson content revisions invalidate outdated slide positions and edited copies so new tasks are not silently skipped. Current saves and custom visual lesson checkpoints continue normally.
