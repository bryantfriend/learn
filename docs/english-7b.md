# 7B conversational English — first week

Choose **7B → Conversational English**. The subject is available only to 7B and is labelled as two 40-minute sessions per week. These are two starter lessons, not a completed year curriculum; no weekdays have been assumed.

1. **Find Your People:** greet, ask about interests, return a question, ask a follow-up and negotiate a shared club idea.
2. **The Pop-up Café:** make a polite request, confirm an order, ask a price, handle an unavailable item and correct a mix-up.

Each core has a 4-minute opener, 8-minute demonstration, 8-minute conversation-card practice, 12-minute application, 5-minute feedback task and 3-minute exit exchange. Timings include spoken rehearsal and role swaps, not just reading slides. Teacher notes explain beginner support and more demanding follow-ups.

Click or tap an underlined word or phrase for a small dialog with its **Simplified Chinese meaning, pinyin, plain English explanation and example**. Vocabulary is authored locally and requires no online translation service. Meanings are specific to the lesson: for example, *server* means the café worker. The dialog can open over a conversation card and closes without replacing it. Escape and Close both work.

Conversation cards have three rounds, visible sentence support, optional example dialogues and teacher-controlled navigation. Students can point and say one word before building a sentence; stronger speakers can hide the support. Invented names/preferences are welcome. No real food, payments, student devices or book are needed.

Optional class/homework sheets are available through Teacher tools, with the café menu included. The existing multilingual independent-study exports remain separate; these new oral lessons are marked `classroomOnly` and excluded from that export until independent-study versions are authored.

Validation: unit tests check class availability, exact timing, vocabulary entries and worksheet sources. Browser tests walk both lessons and check Chinese dialogs, nested conversation cards, touch operation, mobile popup fit and visible-content bounds.
