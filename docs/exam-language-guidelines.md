# Exam language for all grades

Use these rules for Geography and Global Perspectives exams in every grade. The teacher approved the template's formatting and simpler-English A4 PDF sample, then requested the remaining year exams on 22 September 2026.

- Use short sentences, familiar words and direct instructions. Put one task in each sentence where possible.
- Keep the thinking and subject content appropriate for the grade. Simpler English must not reduce the skill being assessed.
- Keep needed subject terms, including perspective, evidence, claim, source, survey, sample, erosion and deposition. Use simpler language around them. Explain unfamiliar everyday words when needed, without giving away an answer.
- Keep important command words such as compare and explain when they match the learning objective. Make the expected response and marks clear.
- Prefer “take part” to “participate,” “use the numbers” to “use the figures,” “made up” to “fictional,” and “make better” to “improve” where the meaning stays the same.
- Avoid idioms, unnecessary abstract words, long clauses and extra negatives. Use the same word for the same idea throughout a question.
- Simplify answer choices as well as questions. Keep choices grammatically parallel and equally clear; do not make the correct answer stand out through language.
- Check that the answer key, marks and assessment objectives remain valid after rewriting.
- Preserve the approved cover, fonts, page structure and answer spaces. Render and inspect every page after edits.

The approved sample is 7A Global Perspectives Term 1. The format is four A4 pages, 50 marks plus 2 bonus, with the reference template's 90-minute duration. The year PDF pack is in `output/year-exams`, with individual student papers and a separate teacher guide. Baselines retain their approved 20-question, one-page format. Generating these PDFs does not publish changes to the app.
